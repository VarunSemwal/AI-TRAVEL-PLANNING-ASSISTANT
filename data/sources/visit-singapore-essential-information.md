# Before You Visit

Singapore is a tropical city-state located near the Equator.

Visitors should prepare for:

- Hot weather
- High humidity
- Occasional heavy rainfall
- Extensive public transportation
- Cashless payment options
- English-friendly communication

---

# Climate & Weather

Singapore has a tropical climate throughout the year.

Typical temperatures are approximately:

- 25°C to 33°C

Weather is generally:

- Hot
- Humid
- Tropical
- Rainy at times

Rain can occur throughout the year and may arrive as short, heavy
thunderstorms.

## What to Pack

Recommended items include:

- Lightweight clothing
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Umbrella
- Sunscreen
- Sunglasses
- Reusable water bottle
- Light rain protection

For long outdoor activities, plan breaks because heat and humidity can make
walking tiring.

---

# Time Zone

Singapore Standard Time:

**GMT +8 / UTC +8**

Singapore does not normally observe daylight-saving time.

---

# Language

Singapore has four official languages:

- English
- Malay
- Mandarin Chinese
- Tamil

English is the common language used in:

- Hotels
- Restaurants
- Shops
- Public transportation
- Government services
- Tourist attractions

Visitors should generally have no difficulty communicating in English.

---

# Singlish

Visitors may also encounter "Singlish", an informal variety of English
influenced by Singapore's different languages and dialects.

It is commonly heard in casual conversations.

---

# Entry & Visa Information

Visa requirements depend on nationality and travel circumstances.

Travellers should verify the latest requirements before departure through
Singapore's official immigration authorities.

Do not rely on outdated visa information from travel blogs or older guides.

---

# Currency

The official currency is the:

**Singapore Dollar (SGD)**

Currency symbol:

**S$**

---

# Payments

Singapore has a highly developed cashless payment environment.

Credit and debit cards are widely accepted at:

- Hotels
- Restaurants
- Shops
- Attractions
- Taxis

Contactless payment is common.

Supported payment methods may include:

- Contactless cards
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Other mobile payment services

However, carrying some cash can still be useful for smaller merchants and
some hawker stalls.

---

# Electricity

Singapore uses approximately:

**220–240 volts AC / 50 Hz**

Power sockets generally use the:

**Type G three-square-pin plug**

This is similar to the electrical plug used in the United Kingdom.

Travellers from countries using different plug types should bring a
universal travel adapter.

---

# Drinking Water

Tap water in Singapore is safe to drink.

Visitors can therefore refill reusable water bottles rather than relying
entirely on bottled water.

---

# Connectivity

Singapore has extensive mobile and internet connectivity.

Visitors can use:

- Mobile data
- Local SIM cards
- eSIMs
- Hotel Wi-Fi
- Public Wi-Fi

---

# Public Wi-Fi

Visitors can access Wireless@SGx hotspots.

Foreign visitors may use the Wireless@SGx service with a foreign mobile
number through the applicable setup process.

Availability and registration requirements can change, so visitors should
check the latest information when arriving.

---

# Public Transportation

Singapore has an extensive public transport system.

Major options include:

- MRT
- Public buses
- Taxis
- Ride-hailing services

The MRT is generally the fastest option for travelling between major
districts.

---

# MRT

The Mass Rapid Transit system connects many important destinations.

It is useful for reaching:

- Marina Bay
- Orchard
- Chinatown
- Little India
- Bugis
- HarbourFront
- Changi Airport connections

---

# Buses

Buses provide extensive coverage beyond the MRT network.

They can be useful for reaching locations farther from train stations.

---

# Taxis & Ride-Hailing

Taxis are widely available.

Ride-hailing services are also commonly used.

Visitors should check fares through the relevant application before booking.

---

# Smoking

Smoking is prohibited in many public locations.

Restrictions apply to areas including:

- Buildings
- Retail food establishments
- Public transportation
- Transport nodes
- Public service vehicles
- Sports facilities

Visitors should always look for designated smoking areas.

---

# Safety

Singapore is generally considered a safe destination.

Nevertheless, visitors should:

- Protect passports and valuables.
- Follow local laws.
- Follow traffic rules.
- Follow public transportation rules.
- Respect smoking restrictions.
- Follow signs and instructions at attractions.
- Be aware of heat and dehydration.

Singapore has strict laws for certain offences, so visitors should
familiarize themselves with local regulations.

---

# Prayer Facilities

Singapore is a Muslim-friendly destination.

Prayer facilities can be found at:

- Mosques
- Some hotels
- Major attractions
- Shopping and commercial areas

Visitors can check with individual venues for prayer-room availability.

---

# Clothing

Singapore's climate generally favours:

- Light
- Breathable
- Comfortable clothing

When visiting religious sites, visitors should dress respectfully and follow
any clothing requirements specified by the venue.

---

# Weather Planning

Because rainfall can be sudden, outdoor itineraries should include some
flexibility.

A useful strategy is to combine:

**Outdoor attraction + indoor attraction**

For example:

- Gardens by the Bay + Marina Bay Sands
- Chinatown + museum
- Sentosa + indoor attractions
- Orchard shopping + restaurants

---

# Public Holidays & Events

Singapore has a busy annual calendar.

Major events and periods include:

- Lunar New Year
- Hari Raya
- Deepavali
- Christmas
- National Day
- Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix
- Major shopping periods
- Christmas season

Hotels and attractions can become busier during major events.

---

# Shopping Hours

Many major shopping malls and tourist attractions operate throughout the
week.

Individual businesses may have different opening hours.

Always check the official website of a specific attraction or business
before visiting.
