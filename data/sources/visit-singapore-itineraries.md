# How to Use This Guide

Singapore is compact and has an efficient transportation system, making it
possible to combine several attractions in one day.

When planning an itinerary, group attractions by geographical area rather
than crossing the city repeatedly.

A typical day can be divided into:

- Morning – outdoor sightseeing
- Afternoon – attractions, shopping or museums
- Evening – food, nightlife or city views

Because Singapore is hot and humid, avoid packing too many outdoor
activities into the middle of the day.

# 1-Day Singapore Itinerary

## Best For

- First-time visitors
- Short stopovers
- Cruise passengers
- Business travellers

## Morning – Marina Bay

Start around Marina Bay.

Suggested attractions:

- Merlion area
- Marina Bay waterfront
- Marina Bay Sands
- Esplanade

Spend the morning exploring the waterfront and taking photographs of the
Singapore skyline.

## Afternoon – Gardens by the Bay

Visit:

- Gardens by the Bay
- Flower Dome
- Cloud Forest
- Supertree Grove

Take a break indoors during the hottest part of the day.

## Evening – Chinatown & Singapore River

Finish the day with:

- Chinatown
- Local food
- Singapore River
- Clarke Quay
