## 🇸🇬 Singapore at a Glance

Singapore is a city-state in Southeast Asia known for its modern skyline,
efficient public transportation, multicultural neighbourhoods, tropical
climate, shopping, nightlife and diverse food culture.

The city combines modern skyscrapers and shopping malls with historic
districts, temples, mosques, churches, hawker centres, parks, beaches and
nature reserves.

Singapore is also an important transportation and business hub for Southeast
Asia and works well as a stopover or starting point for exploring the region.

---

# Districts

Singapore is compact, but its neighbourhoods have very different
characteristics and attractions.

## Singapore CBD

The Central Business District is the modern financial centre of Singapore.

Highlights include:

- Raffles Place
- Singapore River
- Boat Quay
- Marina Bay
- Modern skyscrapers
- Restaurants and bars
- Major business and commercial areas

---

## Riverside / Civic District

The Riverside and Civic District form part of Singapore's historic core.

Highlights include:

- Singapore River
- Boat Quay
- Clarke Quay
- Museums
- Historic buildings
- Theatres
- Restaurants and nightlife

This area is particularly suitable for visitors interested in Singapore's
colonial history and evening entertainment.

---

## Marina Bay

Marina Bay is one of Singapore's most recognizable areas.

Major attractions include:

- Marina Bay Sands
- Gardens by the Bay
- Singapore Flyer
- Esplanade
- Marina Barrage
- Marina Bay waterfront

The area is especially attractive in the evening when the skyline is
illuminated.

---

## Orchard Road

Orchard Road is Singapore's major shopping district.

Visitors will find:

- Large shopping malls
- Luxury brands
- Department stores
- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Hotels

The area is convenient for visitors interested in shopping and
entertainment.

---

## Bugis & Kampong Glam

Bugis and Kampong Glam are associated with Singapore's Malay and Muslim
heritage.

Highlights include:

- Sultan Mosque
- Arab Street
- Haji Lane
- Traditional shops
- Cafés and restaurants
- Boutique stores
- Street art

The area is particularly lively in the evening.

---

## Chinatown

Chinatown is an important Chinese heritage district.

Visitors can explore:

- Historic shophouses
- Chinese temples
- Restaurants
- Hawker centres
- Souvenir shops
- Cultural attractions

Chinatown is also a good area for experiencing Singapore's food culture.

---

## Little India

Little India provides a strong connection to Singapore's Indian heritage.

Highlights include:

- Hindu temples
- Indian restaurants
- Spice shops
- Textile shops
- Traditional businesses
- Colourful streets

Tekka Centre is an important food and market destination in the area.

---

## Sentosa & HarbourFront

Sentosa is a resort island south of Singapore.

Major attractions include:

- Beaches
- Universal Studios Singapore
- Resorts
- Entertainment venues
- Adventure activities
- Mount Faber connections

Sentosa is particularly suitable for families and visitors looking for
entertainment and beach activities.

---

## East Coast

The eastern part of Singapore includes:

- East Coast Park
- Beaches
- Seafood restaurants
- Changi Airport
- Geylang Serai
- Pulau Ubin

Pulau Ubin provides a more rustic contrast to Singapore's highly urban
environment.

---

## North & West

Northern and western Singapore contain residential and industrial areas,
nature reserves and several major attractions.

The Mandai area is particularly important because it contains Singapore's
major wildlife attractions.

---

# Major Attractions

## Gardens by the Bay

A major attraction in Marina Bay combining large landscaped gardens,
conservatories and futuristic Supertree structures.

Important areas include:

- Flower Dome
- Cloud Forest
- Supertree Grove

---

## Marina Bay Sands

A large integrated resort containing:

- Hotel
- Shopping mall
- Restaurants

It is one of the most recognizable landmarks on the Singapore skyline.

## Singapore Flyer

A large observation wheel providing views across Marina Bay and surrounding
Singapore.

---

## Singapore Botanic Gardens

The Singapore Botanic Gardens is a major green space close to the city.

It is also Singapore's UNESCO World Heritage Site.

---

## Mandai Wildlife Reserve

The Mandai area contains several major wildlife attractions, including:

- Singapore Zoo
- Night Safari
- Bird Paradise
- River Wonders

It is one of the best destinations in Singapore for families and wildlife
enthusiasts.

---

## Chinatown

---

## Little India

# Transportation

Singapore has an extensive and efficient public transportation network.

## MRT

The MRT is the backbone of Singapore's public transportation system.

It is:

- Fast
- Reliable
- Affordable
- Air-conditioned
- Connected to major tourist areas

The MRT is generally the preferred method for travelling longer distances
around Singapore.

---

## Bus

Singapore's bus network complements the MRT.

Buses can be particularly useful when:

- The destination is not close to an MRT station.
- You want to see the city while travelling.
- You are travelling through residential neighbourhoods.

Many buses are air-conditioned and wheelchair accessible.

---

## Taxi

Taxis are widely available and use meters.

They can be useful when:

- Travelling with several people
- Carrying luggage
- Travelling late
- Visiting places that are inconvenient by MRT

---

## Ride-Hailing

Ride-hailing applications are widely used in Singapore.

Popular services include:

- Grab
- Gojek
- Ryde
- TADA

Prices can vary according to demand and time of day.

---

## Walking

Singapore is generally pedestrian-friendly.

Walking is particularly enjoyable in:

- Marina Bay
- Chinatown
- Kampong Glam
- Little India
- Civic District
- Orchard

However, Singapore's tropical heat and humidity should be considered when
planning long walks.

---

## Cycling

Cycling paths and shared-use paths are available throughout Singapore.

Shared bicycles can be rented using mobile applications.

# Food

Food is one of Singapore's major attractions.

Singapore's cuisine reflects Chinese, Malay, Indian, Peranakan and other
cultural influences.

## Popular Local Dishes

Visitors should consider trying:

- Hainanese chicken rice
- Laksa
- Char kway teow
- Chilli crab
- Black pepper crab
- Satay
- Nasi lemak
- Roti prata
- Bak kut teh

---

## Hawker Centres

Hawker centres are an important part of Singapore's food culture.

They offer a large variety of affordable local dishes in one location.

Popular examples include:

- Lau Pa Sat
- Newton Food Centre
- Tekka Centre
- Old Airport Road Food Centre
- Tiong Bahru Market

For a more local experience, visitors can explore hawker centres outside
the main tourist areas.

---

## Food Courts

Shopping malls commonly contain air-conditioned food courts.

They offer many of the same types of dishes found in hawker centres, usually
at somewhat higher prices.

---

## Vegetarian Food

Singapore has many vegetarian options.

Indian restaurants and food stalls commonly provide vegetarian dishes, and
Chinese vegetarian restaurants are also available.

Visitors with dietary restrictions should confirm ingredients with the
restaurant or food stall.

# Money & Payments

Singapore uses the Singapore dollar (SGD).

Credit and debit cards are widely accepted.

Contactless payments and mobile wallets are also commonly used.

Cash can still be useful for some smaller food stalls and businesses.

---

# Weather

Singapore has a tropical climate.

Typical conditions include:

- High temperatures
- High humidity
- Frequent rainfall
- Short but heavy rain showers

Visitors should carry water and consider carrying a small umbrella.

---

# Practical Tips

- Singapore is generally very easy to navigate.
- MRT and buses are useful for most sightseeing.
- Carry water when walking outdoors.
- Use contactless payment where accepted.
- Respect local laws and regulations.
- Follow smoking restrictions.
- Keep public areas clean.
- Check attraction opening hours before visiting.
- Consider visiting popular attractions early to avoid crowds.
